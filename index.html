What it runs on

We want to be upfront about how this works. This is an independent community initiative, not a software provider.

Software

Tabbycat

Your tab site is a regular Tabbycat site, the free and open-source tabulation software. We are not officially affiliated with the Tabbycat team.

Live site

Render

The live site is hosted on Render, so your address looks like yourname.onrender.com.

Backup

Vercel

After your tournament, the backup site of your public pages is hosted on Vercel, so its address looks like yourname.vercel.app.

Simple, subsidised pricing

No feature limits

Unlike most free tiers, nothing is held back. Draws, ballots, private URLs, feedback, break rounds, public pages, email and everything else Tabbycat offers.

After your tournament

Your tournament runs fully on the live tab site for the days you've booked. Once it's over, we don't keep the live site running forever.

During your booking

Live tab site

  • The complete site with every feature: draws, ballots, results, feedback and more, for the days you've booked.
  • Your site stays up for 3 more weeks after the tournament ends, after which it is temporarily turned off.
  • For any reference after that, the backup site can be used and viewed.
Afterwards

Permanent backup site

  • A copy of all your public pages (draws, results, standings, breaks, speaker tabs) that looks just like the original.
  • Anyone can open it, anytime, with no waiting.
  • To make changes, please get in touch.

Terms of use

Don't abuse email

Use the site's email only for your tournament. No spam, bulk promotions or unrelated messages.

Real identities

To prevent misuse, do not use a false personal, organiser or tournament identity. All participants must use real, identifiable names.

No registration fees

Subsidised tiers are only for tournaments that do not charge entry fees.
